Received the University President’s Award in the Undergraduate Student-Directed Education Program

I received the University President’s Award on January 16, 2024, following the final evaluation of the 2023 Undergraduate Student-Directed Education Program at Seoul National University.

The program supported year-long, student-led research across academic disciplines, and our research received KRW 5 million in funding. At the end of the program, 30 projects were evaluated on their research outcomes. Award recipients were also eligible for up to KRW 1 million per person in support for a first-author journal publication.
