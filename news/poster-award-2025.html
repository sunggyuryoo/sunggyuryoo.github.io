Received the Best Poster Award at ICFPE 2025

I received the Best Poster Award at the International Conference on Flexible and Printed Electronics (ICFPE 2025) in Tokyo, Japan. The presentation focused on phototransistors based on in situ core/shell perovskite–MoS2 heterostructures.
