Perovskite–MoS2 phototransistor research featured by SNU

Two-dimensional transition metal dichalcogenides (TMDCs) offer a promising platform for compact, flexible phototransistors. However, their extremely thin channels absorb little light, which limits photoresponse.

We used an in situ core/shell perovskite–MoS2 heterojunction to reduce surface defects and improve photocarrier injection. We identified photoconductive and photogating effects as the main operating mechanisms and determined operating conditions for improved photodetection. These results show how combining perovskites with two-dimensional semiconductors can improve phototransistor performance.

Our study was published in ACS Nano and featured on the website of Seoul National University’s Department of Physics and Astronomy.
